# Game design — review draft

Authority: DECISIONS.md; unresolved choices remain OPEN. No game code until DESIGN LOCKED.

## Experience

A desktop-browser, single-player, pausable real-time Deccan campaign sandbox beginning from a researched 1622 snapshot. The player earns a livelihood, establishes relationships and builds power through trade, service, military action and family politics. The world continues independently.

First deliverable: a complete, polished Region Pack 01 campaign, approved by the user on 2026-11-07. Prove the full campaign experience at small geographic scale before adding regions. Historical actors outside the playable area require dated external-interaction content; do not move their capitals or holdings into this pack merely to include them.

Pillars: reproducible living world; people and obligations drive politics; terrain and supply shape conflict; honest provenance; readable decisions with visible consequences.

## Core loop

Travel → inspect opportunities → negotiate or act → spend time/resources → observe consequences → revise plans. Every offer shows cost, timing, risk, obligations and known information.

| Play period | Intended experience |
|---|---|
| First 10 minutes | Choose a clearly labelled background, inspect a settlement, buy supplies, accept one local job, plot travel and meet a character. Backgrounds await content approval. |
| First hour | Complete a delivery or escort, compare prices, recruit a small party, experience an encounter and earn trust or notoriety. |
| First 10 hours | Develop a trade/security network, enter paid service, build a household or capture a holding; see rivals react to remembered actions. |

Careers share systems: trader earns from arbitrage and supply contracts; security provider protects routes; mercenary sells service; noble administers grants and obligations; raider/bandit earns through coercion with pursuit and reputation consequences; ruler builds legitimacy, resources and supporters. Names such as “Pindari” require date-specific research before use as historical starting content.

No universal victory timer. Optional goals measure solvency, household continuity, commercial reach, holdings or political recognition. Defeat can mean capture, lost property or debt rather than immediate termination. Exact permanent death/heir game-over rules remain OPEN. Difficulty changes information, economic margins and AI planning budgets through explicit presets, never hidden historical bonuses.

Identity influences personal priorities only through authored, inspectable factors. Loyalty, ambition, empathy, risk tolerance, honor and greed vary between individuals in every community. No culture/religion is assigned inherent cruelty, intelligence or betrayal probability. Historical figures receive sourced facts separately from uncertain gameplay traits.

## Scope

V1 targets campaign travel, markets, caravans, recruitment, auto-battles, menu sieges, households, diplomacy, succession, companions, generated issues and kingdom creation. These arrive milestone by milestone within Region Pack 01. Separate RTS battles come later. Skip 3D roaming, first-person combat, smithing, arenas and multiplayer. Audio deferred. Exact boundary/activation list, duration and maritime travel await decisions.

Polish means clear readable screens, consistent art, stable frame pacing, reliable saves, explainable AI and satisfying feedback. It is not guaranteed by replacing markers with PNGs; art consistency, readability at zoom and simulation balance require inspection and playtesting.
