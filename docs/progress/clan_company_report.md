# Clan and company management

Party release rows now have quantity sliders and require Done to commit. Sarai offers separate troop return sliders, cancellation and confirmation. Returning troops refunds the listed initial enlistment fee. Foot guards receive 1 coin monthly, mounted scouts 2 and companions 5. The clan treasury pays every 720 simulation hours. Insufficient funds accumulate as visible arrears, and companions remain with the clan.

Clan is available in the bottom navigation or with L. It shows the house, treasury payroll, saved Scout, Quartermaster and Surgeon appointments, and companion parties. Role skill bonuses remain future work. At an anchored settlement, assign an available companion and transfer troops to create a patrol. It travels independently between its home and Saswad. Recall sends it toward the player, and Rejoin merges nearby troops without duplicating them. Each patrol consumes one grain daily from shared clan stores. It currently does not trade, recruit, or select career goals independently.

Companion enlistment is a one-time fee and monthly salary. Named companions have a distinct management role and remain loyal. Additional combat skills and individual battle participation remain future work.

The economy currently links geography, production, monsoon output, physical cargo, route travel time and weekly prices. No new salt or oil historical assumptions were introduced in this change. Coastal fish and rice use the existing preview profiles. War, policies and road security do not yet alter production or supply.

Chrome verified clan navigation, companion hiring, detachment rendering, recall and merge, Sarai refund, and saving and restoring a detachment. The build, 52 tests and data validation passed, with the two existing Panhala warnings.
