# Grain mill and campaign presentation

Pune has a purchasable grain mill. The preview acquisition costs 300 coin, with working capital funded separately. It converts 2 grain to 2 flour over 6 hours and pays 2 coin wages per completed batch. Controls include production pause, automatic input/output trade, price limits, funding/withdrawal and on-site goods transfers. Storage is bounded, output must sell to earn revenue and operating transactions appear separately. Values are gameplay tuning.

Opening an interaction screen pauses the campaign. Closing leaves the world paused until the player resumes. The player uses a portrait when zoomed out and the rider sprite close up. Other parties appear only at closer zoom within a local visibility distance. Native hover text exposes available party facts, with unknown values explicitly unknown. Night and dawn/dusk colors transition smoothly.
