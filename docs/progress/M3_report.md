# M3 first economy and campaign screens

The user authorized campaign UI foundations, market-driven caravan behavior and simple delivery quests. Bottom navigation opens Inventory (I), Party (P), Character (C), Quests (J) and Settlement. Escape closes a screen. Opening a screen pauses the campaign. At the current settlement, visit the market or, in Saswad, talk to a fictional supplier. Buy and sell grain/wood one unit at a time. Accept the request in Saswad and deliver twenty grain and fifty wood before ninety-six game hours pass. The fixed reward pays once.

Three preview markets start with different stocks and the same bounded pricing rule. A fictional caravan chooses a price differential, buys cargo, physically travels using M2 paths, sells on arrival and chooses another opportunity. Prices respond to the stock actually moved. Region production, recipe chains and seasonal demand remain incomplete. Economic values, quest quantities, portrait and dialogue are gameplay assumptions.

Tests cover atomic stock/money transfer, rejected purchases, single quest reward and expiry. A five-year sequence of merchant visits checks bounded stock/prices and conserved trader/market money. This is an economic-transition test. A full hourly five-year campaign soak with production and consumption remains part of M3 acceptance.

Party and Character are initial page foundations. Recruitment, skills, companions, tavern/inn content, workshops, factories, owned caravans and guards require their respective simulation systems. Geographic forest and crossing constraints still require route/terrain research. The current roads remain inferred M2 preview corridors. Reloading starts a fresh session.

The existing polity pennants now have colored settlement rings. Historical sovereignty comes from the record. No precise territorial border is inferred from a ring.
