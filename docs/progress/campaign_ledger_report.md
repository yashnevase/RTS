# Treasury, encyclopedia and companion finances

Coin hover and keyboard focus show the last 7 days of actual treasury changes, estimated payroll per day and month, arrears and up to 8 recent transactions. Food hover lists grain, rice and fish with quantities and daily upkeep. Quest quantities use numerals and goods icons.

Quest and settlement names link to encyclopedia entries. Encyclopedia place names in notes link to their records. Locate on map supports the player, supplier, hired companions and place entries where a location is known. Unlocated historical characters retain their unknown location.

Companion parties receive a starting purse of up to 20 coin and up to 3 grain transferred from the clan. They maintain separate money and food, buy grain from local markets and record a local relation for purchases. Monthly allowance defaults to 10 coin and can be set from 5 through 100. Rejoining returns unused coin and provisions to the clan. Ambition, generosity and aggression values are explicit fictional placeholders. They do not yet choose careers or quests. Autonomous recruitment, intel sales, betrayal, and personality-driven diplomacy remain future work.

Faction influence now uses displayed market seats and forts, removing isolated patches seeded by other catalog locations. It remains an approximate visualization, not verified territorial boundaries.

The build, 52 tests and data validation passed, with 2 existing Panhala region warnings. Chrome verified treasury transaction text, food icons, a quest-to-encyclopedia link, Locate on map and companion budget creation.
