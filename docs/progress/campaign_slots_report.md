# Save slots and campaign clarity

Five local slots support saving, loading, deletion, JSON export and import into an empty slot. Existing single-save data remains available through Continue at startup. Loading and overwriting require confirmation. The browser exports to the download location chosen by the user. Imported JSON receives structural checks before storage.

The rectangular close-up relief layer caused a visible seam against the regional map. That layer was removed. The map uses one continuous regional relief texture with its vegetation and river overlay.

Rice and fish now have regional import modifiers on their base costs. At comparable stock levels Chaul is cheaper, Pune intermediate and Ahmadnagar dearer. These are explicitly tunable gameplay assumptions, not historical price records.

Party count hover lists troop counts and companion names. Follow centers and tracks the company through one control. Speed is shown as an estimate from current terrain, troops, load and health. Authoritative travel continues to use effort sampled along each segment.

Character shows skills, personality, renown, influence and morale. Trading skill grows with completed trades, fighting with encounters, victories earn renown and improve morale, and supply contract completion adds renown and influence. Fighting contributes to the preview encounter calculation. Influence has no political spending action yet. Skills and traits are currently player data; a shared record model for all main NPCs remains future work.

Horses/livestock, docks, workshop production chains and full NPC personality effects remain future modules. A single workshop input/delivery/output loop is recommended before more content or tactical RTS development.

Build, 55 tests and data validation passed, with two existing Panhala warnings. Chrome verified five slots, saving and loading a changed roster, party hover and Character statistics.
